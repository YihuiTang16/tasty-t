# One-minute demo

Record or present against the running site. Do not refresh the data five minutes before you go on stage. The sample trip is saved in the app.

**On screen at the start:** the Tasty T homepage, form empty, no results yet.

## 0:00–0:08 — The problem

"Students without cars do not need another list of restaurants. They need places they can reach from the T stop they already use. Tasty T starts at a campus station."

Gesture at the five starting points and the line colors.

## 0:08–0:18 — Sample trip

Click **Try a Sample Trip** in the header.

"This is Boston University, Asian food, a date night, about $15 to $25, and two hours."

Wait until the list appears. Do not keep talking over the scroll.

## 0:18–0:38 — One card

Point at **Nud Pob**.

"Nud Pob is a Thai restaurant. OpenStreetMap puts it under a tenth of a mile from Boston University Central, on the Green Line B branch. The sentence under the name is the actual score: cuisine, station distance, and the fact that it is a restaurant. Budget says not listed. We did not invent a price."

Click **View on Map**. The map moves to that pin. The start pin is Boston University Central.

## 0:38–0:48 — Transit

Click **Get Transit Directions**.

"This opens Google Maps in transit mode, from the campus station to the restaurant. We do not calculate the ride ourselves, so we do not show a fake travel time."

Close the tab and come back.

## 0:48–1:00 — The engine changes its mind

Click **Study cafe**.

"Same engine, different ask. From Northeastern, one hour, coffee. Tatte is at the Northeastern station."

If you still have two seconds, click **Weekend from Harvard**.

"Under $15, free museums move up, because OpenStreetMap says the fee is no. That is the only kind of budget we will claim."

Stop. Do not open the score details unless someone asks.

## If something misbehaves

- If the map tiles are slow, stay on the card. The transit link does not need the tiles.
- If a name looks misspelled, it is the OpenStreetMap name. "Bejing Cafe" is spelled that way in the source. Do not correct it on stage.
- If Google Maps asks for location permission, deny it. The origin is already in the link.

## What you are claiming

- 3,446 places and 125 subway and light rail stations, retrieved November 21, 2026.
- A weighted score you can open on any card.
- No accounts, no paid API, and no made-up prices or ride times.

## What you are not claiming

- That Nud Pob is the best Thai food in Boston.
- That the straight-line distance is how long the train takes.
- That the MBTA endorses the project.
